About Me

A passionate graphic designer with a keen eye for detail and a love for creative storytelling. With experience in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, I specialize in crafting visually engaging designs for book cover and interior design, social media post, poster, branding, and print materials. I hold a degree in Information Technology from the Asian College of Technology, where I developed both my technical and creative skills. I also have experience in web design and coding, allowing me to create visually appealing and functional digital experiences. Over the years, I’ve gained hands-on industry experience. Beyond work, I enjoy character design, traditional and digital drawing, and exploring new design techniques to refine my craft. I’m always eager to learn, grow, and collaborate on exciting projects that bring ideas to life.
